\section*{Section S1: Mathematical Derivation of Bayes-Optimal Logit Shift under Weighted Binary Cross-Entropy}
In this section, we provide the complete formal derivation demonstrating that positive-class weighting in binary cross-entropy (BCE) mathematically induces an additive constant logit shift of $+\ln w$ in the population Bayes-optimal classifier.

Let $X \in \mathcal{X}$ denote the input feature representation (e.g., chest radiograph), and let $Y \in \{0, 1\}$ represent the binary pathology label. Let $\eta(x) = P(Y = 1 \mid X = x)$ denote the true posterior probability under the data-generating distribution. A neural network outputs a scalar logit $z(x) \in \mathbb{R}$, yielding predicted probability $\hat{p}(x) = \sigma(z(x)) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-z(x)}}$.

The positive-weighted binary cross-entropy loss with positive scalar weight $w > 0$ for a single instance $(x, y)$ is defined as:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L}_{\text{pos}}(y, \hat{p}(x)) = - w \cdot y \ln(\hat{p}(x)) - (1 - y) \ln(1 - \hat{p}(x))
\label{eq:supp_loss_def}
\end{equation}

Under population risk minimization, the optimal model minimizes the conditional expected risk at each point $x$:
\begin{align}
R(z(x)) &= \mathbb{E}_{Y \mid X=x} [\mathcal{L}_{\text{pos}}(Y, \sigma(z(x)))] \nonumber \\
&= - w \cdot \eta(x) \ln(\sigma(z(x))) - (1 - \eta(x)) \ln(1 - \sigma(z(x)))
\label{eq:supp_expected_risk}
\end{align}

Recall the standard identity for the logistic sigmoid derivative with respect to its logit $z$:
\begin{equation}
\frac{d}{dz} \sigma(z) = \sigma(z)(1 - \sigma(z))
\end{equation}
Differentiating the conditional expected risk with respect to logit $z(x)$:
\begin{align}
\frac{d R}{dz} &= - w \cdot \eta(x) \frac{\sigma(z)(1 - \sigma(z))}{\sigma(z)} - (1 - \eta(x)) \frac{-\sigma(z)(1 - \sigma(z))}{1 - \sigma(z)} \nonumber \\
&= - w \cdot \eta(x)(1 - \sigma(z)) + (1 - \eta(x))\sigma(z) \nonumber \\
&= \sigma(z) \left[ w \cdot \eta(x) + (1 - \eta(x)) \right] - w \cdot \eta(x)
\label{eq:supp_first_order}
\end{align}

Setting the first-order condition $\frac{d R}{dz} = 0$ to find the optimal predicted probability $\hat{p}^*(x) = \sigma(z^*(x))$:
\begin{equation}
\sigma(z^*(x)) \left[ w \cdot \eta(x) + 1 - \eta(x) \right] = w \cdot \eta(x)
\end{equation}
Solving directly for $\hat{p}^*(x)$:
\begin{equation}
\hat{p}^*(x) = \frac{w \cdot \eta(x)}{w \cdot \eta(x) + (1 - \eta(x))}
\label{eq:supp_opt_prob}
\end{equation}

Converting optimal probability $\hat{p}^*(x)$ into the Bayes-optimal logit representation $z^*(x) = \text{logit}(\hat{p}^*(x))$:
\begin{align}
z^*(x) &= \ln\left( \frac{\hat{p}^*(x)}{1 - \hat{p}^*(x)} \right) \nonumber \\
&= \ln\left( \frac{\frac{w \cdot \eta(x)}{w \cdot \eta(x) + (1 - \eta(x))}}{1 - \frac{w \cdot \eta(x)}{w \cdot \eta(x) + (1 - \eta(x))}} \right) \nonumber \\
&= \ln\left( \frac{w \cdot \eta(x)}{1 - \eta(x)} \right) \nonumber \\
&= \ln\left( \frac{\eta(x)}{1 - \eta(x)} \right) + \ln(w)
\label{eq:supp_bayes_logit_final}
\end{align}

Let $z_{\text{true}}(x) = \text{logit}(\eta(x)) = \ln\left(\frac{P(Y=1 \mid X=x)}{P(Y=0 \mid X=x)}\right)$ denote the true population log-odds. We obtain the exact closed-form relation:
\begin{equation}
z^*(x) = z_{\text{true}}(x) + \ln(w)
\label{eq:supp_exact_shift}
\end{equation}
Equation~(\ref{eq:supp_exact_shift}) proves that positive loss weighting acts as an exact additive translation of the population Bayes-optimal logit by $+\ln w$. When $w > 1$ (standard practice to combat acute class imbalance), uncalibrated predictions suffer an artificial positive logit inflation, which systematically distorts posterior probability calibration into acute over-confidence. Crucially, subtracting the constant offset $\ln w$ ($z \mapsto z - \ln w$) recovers the undistorted log-odds $z_{\text{true}}(x)$ under ideal asymptotic conditions, establishing the theoretical foundation for the $O(1)$ zero-retraining analytic recalibration evaluated in the main manuscript.

\section*{Section S3: Diagnostic Study-Level Sensitivity Analysis}
To evaluate sensitivity to repeated within-patient examinations and multi-image studies, Table~\ref{tab:supp_study_level} summarizes external validation performance aggregated strictly at the diagnostic study level ($N = 3,041$ studies across $N = 289$ unique patients). In accordance with standard radiological diagnostic practice, multiple views acquired during a single examination are aggregated using maximum probability pooling ($\max_i p_i$, where detection on any view indicates study-level positive status). Supplementary Table~\ref{tab:supp_study_level} evaluates all 16 architecture-loss-pathology combinations with patient-clustered 1,000-replicate bootstrap 95\% confidence intervals. As shown, study-level results demonstrate that the principal findings were qualitatively concordant regardless of whether the statistical unit is the individual radiograph or the diagnostic study: unweighted models yield comparable discrimination across views, while external discrimination remains pathology-dependent (AUROC approaching chance for Cardiomegaly), and analytic logit recalibration ($z - \log w$) on positive-weighted models yields statistically significant reductions in study-level Brier error for Cardiomegaly ($\Delta\text{Brier} = -0.0601$ on DenseNet-121 and $-0.0642$ on ResNet-50) and Pneumonia ($\Delta\text{Brier} = -0.0100$ and $-0.0163$, both intervals strictly below zero), alongside consistent point-estimate reductions for Pleural Effusion ($-0.0102$ and $-0.0118$).

\section*{Section S7: Reproducibility and Code Execution Guide}
The complete codebase is organized to support deterministic replication from raw prediction archives through all tables, figures, and statistical tests:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Environment Setup:} Python 3.10+ with PyTorch, torchvision, scikit-learn, scipy, pandas, numpy, and matplotlib.
    \item \textbf{Stage 3 Recalibration Execution:}
    \begin{quote}
    \texttt{python3 scripts/run\_stage3\_recalibration.py}
    \end{quote}
    Computes probability ensembles ($\bar{p}_m = \frac{1}{3}\sum_{s=1}^3 p_{m,s}$) and ensemble logit offsets ($\Delta z = \frac{1}{3}\sum_{s=1}^3 (z_{w,s} - z_{u,s})$) across all 8,302 held-out radiographs (16,604 paired image-architecture evaluations, alongside all 49,812 seed-specific pairs) for raw, temperature-scaled, analytic offset ($z - \log w$), fitted offset ($z + a^*$), and Platt-scaled models, running 2,000-replicate patient-clustered bootstrap inference.
    \item \textbf{Publication Artifact Compilation:}
    \begin{quote}
    \texttt{python3 scripts/compile\_publication\_artifacts.py}
    \end{quote}
    Generates Tables 1--4 in LaTeX and markdown formats, alongside publication figures (Figures 2--6).
    \item \textbf{Verification Audit:}
    \begin{quote}
    \texttt{python3 scripts/audit\_cmpb.py}
    \end{quote}
    Verifies all formatting, word count, highlight length ($\le 85$ characters), and statutory compliance rules for Computer Methods and Programs in Biomedicine.
\end{enumerate}
