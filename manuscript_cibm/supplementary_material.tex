\section*{Section S2: Diagnostic Study-Level Sensitivity Analysis}
To evaluate sensitivity to repeated within-patient examinations and multi-image studies, Table~\ref{tab:supp_study_level} summarizes external validation performance aggregated strictly at the diagnostic study level ($N = 3,041$ studies across $N = 289$ unique patients). In accordance with standard radiological diagnostic practice, multiple views acquired during a single examination are aggregated using maximum probability pooling ($\max_i p_i$, where detection on any view indicates study-level positive status). Supplementary Table~\ref{tab:supp_study_level} evaluates all 16 architecture-loss-pathology combinations with patient-clustered 1,000-replicate bootstrap 95\% confidence intervals. As shown, study-level results demonstrate that all primary findings are invariant to whether the statistical unit is the individual radiograph or the diagnostic study: unweighted models maintain robust discrimination across views, while analytic logit recalibration ($z - \log w$) on positive-weighted models yields statistically significant reductions in study-level Brier error for Cardiomegaly ($\Delta\text{Brier} = -0.0601$ on DenseNet-121 and $-0.0642$ on ResNet-50) and Pneumonia ($\Delta\text{Brier} = -0.0100$ and $-0.0163$, both intervals strictly below zero), alongside consistent point-estimate reductions for Pleural Effusion ($-0.0102$ and $-0.0118$).

\section*{Section S3: Reproducibility and Code Execution Guide}
The complete codebase is organized to support deterministic replication from raw prediction archives through all tables, figures, and statistical tests:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Environment Setup:} Python 3.10+ with PyTorch, torchvision, scikit-learn, scipy, pandas, numpy, and matplotlib.
    \item \textbf{Stage 3 Recalibration Execution:}
    \begin{quote}
    \texttt{python3 scripts/run\_stage3\_recalibration.py}
    \end{quote}
    Computes probability ensembles ($\bar{p}_m = \frac{1}{3}\sum_{s=1}^3 p_{m,s}$) across all 8,302 prospective radiographs (16,604 paired model evaluations) for raw, temperature-scaled, analytic offset ($z - \log w$), fitted offset ($z + a^*$), and Platt-scaled models, running 2,000-replicate patient-clustered bootstrap inference.
    \item \textbf{Publication Artifact Compilation:}
    \begin{quote}
    \texttt{python3 scripts/compile\_publication\_artifacts.py}
    \end{quote}
    Generates Tables 1--4 in LaTeX and markdown formats, alongside publication figures (Figures 2--6).
    \item \textbf{Verification Audit:}
    \begin{quote}
    \texttt{python3 scripts/audit\_cibm.py}
    \end{quote}
    Verifies all formatting, word count, highlight length ($\le 85$ characters), and statutory compliance rules for Computers in Biology and Medicine.
\end{enumerate}
